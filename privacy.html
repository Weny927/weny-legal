Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Weny app handles information when you create baby sticker stories. Weny keeps that work on the device you are using. It does not ask you to create an online account, and it does not upload your photos, drafts, stories, or support notes to a Weny server.

What Weny is

Weny lets you choose a story template, add a baby photo, place stickers, write a short caption, and save a finished story card. You can also dictate the caption and then choose whether to use that text. Finished stories stay in the library on this device. Log Out returns you to the welcome screen and leaves those stories in place.

Information stored on this device

Weny saves the following in its own storage on this device:

  • Copies of photos you take or import for a story.
  • Drafts, including the template, background, sticker positions, caption, font, and whether the photo is shown as a circle.
  • Finished story images and the date each one was saved.
  • The Story Credit balance on this device, and a local record of a completed Story Pack purchase so the same purchase is not added twice. That record includes the pack name, the price shown in the app, and the transaction identifier.
  • Support notes you choose to save.
  • Your light or dark appearance choice, and whether you have left the welcome screen.

This information is used to show your stories, finish a render, and remember a purchase that has already been granted. Weny does not use it to build an advertising profile.

Camera

The camera is used when you tap Take Photo while making a story. The system asks for permission at that time. The picture is saved as a copy inside the app so it can appear on the story card. Weny does not open the camera when you launch the app.

Photo library

The photo library is used when you tap Import from Photos or when you save a finished story image. Importing stores a copy of the photo you select. Saving writes the finished card into your photo library after you ask for that. If photo access is off, you can still make a sticker story, and the finished image remains inside Weny. Clearing app data does not delete the original photos that stay in your library outside the app.

Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone and speech recognition are used when you tap the microphone beside a story line. Weny listens for a short phrase and turns it into text. The words appear so you can keep them or discard them before they are written into the story. Weny does not save a separate audio recording of that phrase, and it does not send the recording to a Weny server.

Purchases

Story Packs are consumable purchases processed by Apple through the App Store. The packs shown in Weny are Starter Gift at $0.99, Keepsake Pack at $2.99, Weekend Bundle at $4.99, Sunday Chest at $7.99, Milestone Chest at $9.99, Family Bundle at $19.99, Studio Pack at $49.99, and Archive Chest at $99.99. Apple handles the payment. After a verified purchase, Weny adds Story Credits on this device. Those credits stay on this device. They are removed if you clear all data or delete the app. Weny does not receive your card number.

Support notes

A note you save in Support stays on this device. It is not sent to a support team. Avoid putting passwords or other secrets in a note if other people can open this device.

Log Out and Clear All Data

Log Out only returns you to the welcome screen. Your stories, drafts, photos, notes, and Story Credits remain on the device. Clear All Data permanently removes stories, drafts, in-app photo copies, support notes, purchase records, and remaining Story Credits from this device. That action cannot be undone inside the app.

Children

Weny is a tool for an adult making a keepsake about a baby. It does not create a child account or a public profile. A photo of a child that you add remains in the app until you delete that story or clear the app data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new text will be published on this page and shown in Weny. Using the app after the page changes means the current text applies.
