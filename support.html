Support

Weny is a baby sticker story app that keeps your photos, drafts, stories, and notes on this device. There is no online account and no Weny server for story content.

In the app

Open Settings and tap Support to leave a note on this device. Those notes stay local and are not sent out by Weny.

Purchases

Story Packs are consumable App Store purchases. Credits stay on this device. If a purchase does not finish, check your App Store account and try again. Weny does not offer a Restore Purchases button for consumable credits.
