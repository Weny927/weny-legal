The app

Weny lets you make a baby sticker story on your own device. You choose a template, take or import a photo, add stickers and a short caption, and render a story image. You can dictate the caption and then keep or discard the text. Finished stories stay in the library on that device. Weny does not publish your stories to other people, and it does not require an online account.

Story Credits

Rendering a new story image uses 1 Story Credit. The review screen shows that cost and your current balance before you confirm. Canceling while the image is being written, or a failed write, does not use a Story Credit. Saving the same finished image again does not use another Story Credit. Editing a story and rendering it again uses 1 Story Credit when the new image is written successfully.

Story Credits are kept only on this device. They have no cash value and cannot be moved to another device.
